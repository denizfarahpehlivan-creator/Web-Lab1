Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world above sea level. It is located on the border between Nepal and China in the Himalayas. Its official height is 8,848.86 meters.

K2

K2 is the second-highest mountain in the world. It is located on the border between Pakistan and China in the Karakoram mountain range. K2 has an elevation of 8,611 meters.

Kangchenjunga

Kangchenjunga is the third-highest mountain in the world. It is located on the border between Nepal and India in the Himalayas. Kangchenjunga has an elevation of 8,586 meters.
